# Governance

On 2026-09-09, the operator authorized creation of `givemedeath/SRN_HAKS` as the public downstream
content repository contemplated by SR_NWN decision D58.

This authorization does not change SR_NWN's permanent private posture and does not grant blanket
permission to export its contents. Every batch landing here must independently clear trademark,
copyright, license, attribution, and redistribution review before public conveyance.

Repository maintainers may reject or remove content whose rights evidence is incomplete. No license
found means do not copy.
