Each head is a rigid 20,000-triangle MDL with a 1024×1024 PLT. These are the exact native bytes the private NWN:EE client
loaded in each head's final review session, taken from that session's trial HAK; publication reruns nothing. 28 heads use the
built-in organic head material; the 12 whose PLT mixes accessory channels keep the legacy head material (`nativeMaterial` in
the manifest). Irises are on tattoo1; orc tusks are on tattoo2. Cyberware is on metal1.
Stock material and palette payloads are not redistributed.

The user selected the designs and delegated operator reviews through the client in batches of five. For each head the
operator checked four three-quarter views (front and back, both sides), a gameplay-distance view, talk/look motion and a
second palette look, then verified the logged slots and colours. Geometry approvals, attempt-budget raises and per-head
threshold exceptions were the user's decisions; each is quoted in that head's `reviewNotes` in the
[portable manifest](head-library-40-v1.json), which also holds resource hashes, design hashes, submission counters, the
client session and trial HAK, and the hash of every source frame used in the galleries.

## User-accepted exceptions and known limitations

- **Quality exceptions:** seamStep Foxglove 1.80, Kestrel 1.93, Sundial 1.90 and Bluehour 2.15 (each in the bake);
  brows-on-hair Dockstone 0.65, Tinlark 0.67, Kestrel 0.71, Nettle 0.755, Foxglove 0.76, Copperleaf 0.77, Silverfern 0.78,
  Farsky 0.82, Copperjaw 0.82 and Breakline 0.84 (brows read whole in the client); Copperleaf hair-on-face-skin 0.04.
  The shared limits are unchanged.
- **Geometry:** Garnet keeps a skin speck high on the back right, a dark pocket low on the back left and a small pale spot
  under each ear; Quartz has 19 tunnels hidden under the bob; Nightshift keeps rear curl gaps (genus 23) under a 0.45%
  surface bound; Nightbloom keeps a small coil hole behind one ear; Copperleaf keeps a few crown braid gaps and a chin crease.
  All were judged in the client.
- **Colour:** Nightbloom's smoky eye sockets are softened, not removed. Copperleaf's darkest braid crevices read as dark
  patches on the back of the head. Copperjaw has one tiny pale fleck above the right ear.
  Quartz's bake mottling was cleared with `palette.skinDetailContrast` before her final review.
- **Cyberware:** metal1 shows the armour's metal colour. Without armour the client uses stock row 0, which every metal row
  draws with environment shine, so plates and implants read olive or teal on unarmoured characters.

Helmet combinations, every palette row, the full animation catalogue, extended gameplay and multiplayer were not checked.
Each head was checked in an isolated trial HAK with its batch; the combined production pack has not had a client session.

## Loading

Load `srn_head` above `srn_body` and use the slots above. All 136 existing resources remain byte-identical. No bodies,
tables, pack registration or existing head slots change. This is a publication PR candidate, not a server deployment.
