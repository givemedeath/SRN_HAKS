# Current NWN:EE purpose-built part workflow

The current Human phenotype pipeline generates isolated parts through the running local ComfyUI `SR_NWN_3d_pixal3d_multi_views` workflow and replaces actual stock parts one at a time. The kit's original Meshy whole-body generation/slicing instructions remain available for older and general workflows. Their blanket whole-body-only rule, three-view/light-grey recipe, automatic width/depth rig fitting and generic joint fractions do not govern this NWN pipeline.

Start with the [current runbook](phenotype-purpose-built-current-runbook.md), [foot checkpoint](phenotype-foot-checkpoint.md), [material validation](phenotype-body-texture-repair-validation.md), [production process](phenotype-purpose-built-production.md) and [part-generation procedure](phenotype-purpose-built-part-generation.md). Current foot regeneration is authorized and supersedes the app-restart stop. Latest direction uses normal closed ankle ends in six orthographic references, then measured post-fit taper if needed, with restrained baked shading for definition. Chest, pelvis, thighs and shins retain their selected geometry and effective combined-v2 materials. Default validation collects parts offline for later bulk native/client checks; the completed material task's client tests do not establish anatomy acceptance of a newly generated foot. Finish feet, then await instructions. Whole-body production gates remain separate.

1. Measure the actual stock part, attachment pivots, caps and animated overlap. Preserve Human male stock height, supermodel, animation chain, neck/head and identity equipment. Kit joint fractions, automatic rig stretching and former custom limbs are not replacement targets. Kit `thigh_l` maps to NWN `legl`/`lthigh_g`, `thigh_r` to `legr`/`rthigh_g`, `shin_l` to `shinl`/`lshin_g` and `shin_r` to `shinr`/`rshin_g`. Do not infer the stock side from the kit's positive-X convention.
2. Design one coherent isolated part with the correct anatomy and ownership. Normalize images over black with one shared isotropic scale: the four-view branch top-aligns, while the six-view orthographic branch centers complete silhouettes and includes genuine top/sole cameras. Save source, prompt, normalized panels and provenance in the user's `reference_images/normalized` folder. Do not independently stretch views, include neighbouring anatomy or assume labels prove orientation.
3. Pass the explicit part configuration to `generate_purpose_built_part.py`; prepare now rejects a missing configuration. Frozen helpers from completed attempts retain their executed behavior. Prepare freezes the live workflow/schema/views and executed dependencies. Submit once, preserve the receipt and collect with status. Keep raw, remeshed, compact and textured masters. Closed-end imagery and filtered UDF do not themselves prove closed topology.
4. Exhaust proper rotation, uniform scale and placement against stock attachments before bounded local edits. Preserve muscle mass, original maps and authored attributes outside the declared edit. Record topology, winding, genuine intersections, cap ownership and pose evidence. Head/neck are separate stock parts. Generation poses must never alter supermodels or animation controllers.
5. Bind the fitted/conditioned receipt and source hash, then extract original embedded 2K maps through their actual bindings. The adapter's skin0 policy does not prove an image contains only skin. Measure visible native skin against accepted neighbours; a common palette alone does not ensure continuity. Follow [native material preservation](phenotype-body-material-process.md): AO is moderated into PLT shade, ORM green is extracted to texture3 with explicit Roughness 0, and normal pixels/strength and fixed cloth remain unchanged. Original compiler inputs and effective runtime material descendants have separate receipts. Current helpers require explicit supported atlas/UV layouts; samplers and double-sided behavior need inspection. Test effective shaders, palettes, connector bands and lighting before selecting; do not drop AO/roughness again when composing the next part.
6. Perfect a left donor before mirroring. Use the actual stock joint frames, reflect geometry only, reverse triangle winding and tangent handedness, and preserve material sampling. `prepare_stock_limb_stage_config.py` independently checks the reflected P/N/UV/T data and rejects merely relabeling a left candidate as right. Then independently review the opposite attachments and animations.
7. Collect each finished offline part with hash-bound pose, topology and material proof. `compose_stock_limb_offline.py` keeps a clean declared body collection separate from comparator/test fixtures and records unexecuted native work; its mixed ASCII/native HAK is diagnostic. Shin runs preserve `chest,pelvis,legl,legr`; feet also preserve `shinl,shinr`, verified by `stock_limb_contract.py`. Bulk native compilation and client validation follow the phenotype collection after the user releases access. `compose_stock_limb_native.py` preserves accepted native bytes and verifies independent compiler receipts. Audit actual HAK/MOD payloads, dependencies, rig/height, palettes, actors and empty override; save unlocked-camera evidence tied to package hashes. Offline selection does not claim native/client acceptance.

The kit's preview inspection and ownership checks remain useful principles. Its generic Meshy scripts and A/T-pose slicing rigs are preserved for their original workflows; do not invoke them as hidden defaults for the current purpose-built Human pipeline. Other races and NWNArmory resizing require explicit measured target/profile work rather than reusing Human resize experiments.

Inspect toe anatomy from the sole and oblique underside before fitting. A closed
mesh can still have an extra digit or touching toe surfaces. Compare the raw,
undecimated and compact masters when a result looks deformed; more polygons
cannot restore anatomy already missing before reduction. Directional reference
lighting and mild baked creases can help texture definition, but heavy shadows
can become false grooves in reconstruction. Keep anatomical geometry broad and
natural, inspect actual maps, and moderate source AO separately into native skin
shades with connector protection. Never invent a dedicated native AO binding.

After a local topology patch, a small coherent UV chart and correct tangent
direction do not establish material continuity. Sample actual same-position
boundary colors and inspect the patch under neutral lighting. The rejected v2
foot chart passed geometry/T tests but differed by up to 58/255 at its boundary.
Do not carry such a mapping forward merely because its UV span is small.
