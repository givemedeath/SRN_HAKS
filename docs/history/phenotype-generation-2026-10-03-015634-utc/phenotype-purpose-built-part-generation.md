## Prior evidence and its limits

The earlier Troll hand used four rendered views of an already-flawed sliced hand; it reproduced fused digits and added noise. That test did not supply newly designed anatomy. Earlier prepared Troll head and upper-arm sheets did use imagegen. The head invented horns, and the closed upper arm remained lumpy with bright shoulder-bake patches after a 1,500-triangle reconstruction. These reject those specific candidates. They do not establish that every independently designed part will fail, or that the 1,500-triangle result fairly represents a new larger torso with measured attachment envelopes and a retained detailed source.

The supplied image-to-parts kit README's general claim that an individual part request necessarily produces a whole figure is a heuristic from its Meshy workflow, not a restriction of the observed local Pixal3D input schema. Its splitting tools remain useful for inherited donors and caps. Purpose-built parts still require measured attachment fitting, coherent material palettes, closed geometry, controlled performance and native/client motion tests; independent generation does not make these automatic.

No client test, native package acceptance or quality improvement is claimed by this preparation. Compare new torso front/side/back/overhead, both clay and color, at equal framing against the current candidate and stock. Check shoulder/neck/waist coverage through idle, casting and crouching before adoption.

## Read-only paired-surface diagnosis

`inspect_generated_part_topology.py` reads the actual GLB arrays, interprets exact coordinate duplicates as UV splits only in diagnostic memory, and uses vectorized edge/ray tests plus near-linear union-by-rank component analysis. It does not alter any model. The detailed 1.52-million-triangle source inspection takes approximately 5.5 seconds with bundled NumPy; SciPy is not required. Frozen code, source hashes, actual topology and depth/vertical ray sections are preserved under `purposebuilt-torso-pilot-v1/paired-surface-diagnostic-v2`.

The thin paired walls already exist in the pre-decimation remeshed master. Its volume is 0.0092909728 versus 0.0092613867 after decimation, a reduction of 0.3184%. Central depth rays intersect four faces with alternating outward/inward geometric normals: wall thickness approximately 0.0055–0.0084 generator units surrounds a cavity approximately 0.275–0.448 units deep. Several vertical rays pass through the central neck-to-waist channel without hitting an end cap. This is actual paired geometry, not merely a normal-map or vertex-normal illusion. At seventy sampled intersections, exported normal directions agree with geometric normals (minimum dot 0.9766, median 0.9990).

The detailed master has six boundary and thirty-five nonmanifold edges; both compact versions have six and one respectively. Each is one coordinate-connected component. The six boundary edges occupy one tiny side-abdominal loop at approximately X=-0.205, Y=-0.189, Z=0.115; they are not the visible large neck or waist opening. An object can have a joined annular rim and a hollow channel without exposing a large topological boundary. Consistent winding and positive volume alone do not establish a usable solid torso. The compact painted and textured meshes have exactly identical positions and oriented triangles after diagnostic coordinate matching, so UV unwrapping/textured export did not create these features.

The inspected Comfy remesh implementation uses `udf - eps`, where `eps = band * scale / resolution`, and explicitly describes the resulting double surface. Its inverted/enclosed filtering removes separate connected components; it cannot remove an inner surface attached to the exterior through a rim within the same component. The observed thin walls are consistent with UDF inflation of an open torso surface. **No raw pre-remesh decoded mesh was saved**, however, so these outputs do not conclusively isolate image-model open ends from remesh behavior. This evidence does establish that final 50K decimation and texturing did not originate the hollow structure. The first fitting pass remains scaling/placement only; no source cleanup or further generation was performed for this diagnosis.
